How these people have shaped my music

Doc Watson

Deeply inspires my storytelling. It is incredible to hear all the fiction he tells so well, and it truly feels like you can relate to anything he sings about.

Bert Jansch

A folk icon. He made tons of classics and almost everyone has covered a Bert Jansch song. His use of thirds gives the songs so much feeling.

Nick Drake

My most consisent aspiry. Its hard to describe how much I love his music. He takes you to a scenic wonderland while playing the most rythimically complex guitar parts.

Bob Dylan

Bob Dylan has alwyas been a feel good artist to me. It's not too complex what he does but its invigorating, and I take notes on his chorus writing. He also makes me want to learn harmonica.

If you're interested, I am on a music creation site called BandLab. It is open source and anyone can listen or make music on here. However you may have to create an account to use it.
